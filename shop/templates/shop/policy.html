Exchange, Return & Cancellation Policy

Exchange & Return Policy

Made Just for You:

Every Romina-Creation outfit is custom-made per order, so we do not offer general returns or exchanges.

Defective Items Only:

Items cannot be returned, but they can be exchanged if they arrive damaged or defective. For orders inside Pakistan, you will need to ship the item back to us at your own delivery cost.

Inspection & Time Limit:

We can only process an exchange after our quality team receives and checks the returned parcel. You must report any issue to us within 7 days of receiving your order.

Order Cancellations

When You Cancel:

You can cancel your order anytime before we start processing it. If you cancel after processing has begun, any advance payment is non-refundable.

When We Cancel:

Romina-Creation can cancel orders due to stock issues or price errors. If you paid online, we will give you a refund or store credit.

Refund Method:

Refunds will be sent back directly to the card you used to make the purchase (note: advance payments are still non-refundable).

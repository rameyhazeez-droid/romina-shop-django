About Romina Creation

Founded in 2020 on Facebook, Romina Creation started as a passion project and has blossomed into a trusted online destination for luxury children's wear.

Our creative journey is led by our CEO and Founder, Aesha Khalid. Although she holds a Master's degree in Statistics, Aesha’s true childhood passion has always been fashion and clothing design. Driven by this love, she began sketching and crafting her own unique styles. Today, those original designs are not only loved locally in Bahawalpur, but have also found a home internationally across the USA, Australia, the UK, UAE, and KSA, winning the hearts of families worldwide.

What We Offer

We design what looks beautiful, trendy, and entirely appropriate for little ones. Specializing in fancy and traditional wear for children aged 0 to 14 years, we aim to turn your little girl into a real-life princess. Supported by a skilled and dedicated professional team, we ensure every stitch is a work of art.

Our Core Values

  • Customer First: Your complete satisfaction and happiness remain our highest priority at all times.
  • Trust & Transparency: We operate firmly on honesty, fair pricing, secure payment methods, and dedicated customer care.
  • Prompt Delivery: Our optimized order processing ensures your packages are safely dispatched without delays.

At Romina Creation, we strive to bring you a magical shopping experience—both online and offline—because you and your children deserve nothing but the best.
